Terms & Conditions of Stay
Smoke-free · Tobacco-free · Alcohol-free property Smoking of cigarettes or bidis, use of tobacco, and consumption of gutka, pan, pan masala or alcohol are strictly prohibited in all areas of the property, including guest rooms, balconies, corridors and common areas. Any violation will attract an immediate fine of ₹2,000.
  1. IdentificationA valid government-issued photo ID is mandatory for every guest, including accompanying guests. Foreign nationals must present a passport and valid visa.
  2. Arrival & departureCheck-in from 11:00 AM and check-out by 9:00 AM. Early arrival and late departure are subject to availability and may be charged.
  3. VisitorsOnly registered guests may stay overnight. Visitors are welcome in the reception area only.
  4. PaymentThe room tariff is payable in advance at check-in. All incidental charges are to be settled before departure.
  5. Dietary observanceOut of respect for the sanctity of Sri Dham Mayapur, non-vegetarian food and eggs are not permitted on the premises.
  6. In-room safetyCooking is not permitted in rooms. Heaters, irons and other high-power appliances may be used only with the approval of the Front Office.
  7. Quiet hoursGuests are requested to keep noise to a minimum, particularly between 10:00 pm and 6:00 am.
  8. Property careAny damage to rooms, furnishings, linen or fittings will be charged to the guest at actual cost.
  9. ValuablesGuests are responsible for their own cash, jewellery and belongings. The management accepts no liability for loss or theft.
  10. Energy & keysPlease switch off lights, fans and air-conditioning when leaving the room, and deposit the room key at the Front Office.
  11. Restricted itemsPets, weapons and illegal substances are not permitted. Any unlawful activity will be reported to the authorities.
  12. Right of admissionThe management reserves the right to refuse or terminate a stay, without refund, in case of misconduct or breach of these terms.
