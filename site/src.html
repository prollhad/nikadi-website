Meet Chandramukhi

Chandramukhi Paul has lived in Sri Dham Mayapur since she was four years old. She grew up with the temple bells, the kirtans and the festivals of the dham, and she is a devotee at heart, so prayer and temple life are part of her every day.

She is also a digital creator and Indian ethnic wear and jewellery model with a love for the luxury lifestyle, and she balances the two beautifully. She has a look for every occasion: a graceful saree for the morning temple program, festive lehengas and gopi dresses for the big temple festivals, and traditional Bengali looks for Durga Pujo, Lakshmi Puja and Diwali.

Her posts reach more than a million people every month, and nearly 29,000 followers come to her for festival outfits, temple jewellery and a glimpse of life in Mayapur. Planning what to wear for your visit? Her page is the perfect place to start.

Questions

Good to know

What are the check-in and check-out times?
Check-in is from 11:00 AM and check-out is by 9:00 AM. Gold members and above get early check-in from 9 AM when the room is available.
How do I confirm my booking?
Send your dates on WhatsApp or use the booking bar at the top. Once we confirm the room is available, pay a 50% advance by UPI, card or bank transfer. The balance is paid at check-in. See how to book and pay.
Can I cancel?
Yes. Cancel 3 or more days before check-in and we refund your full advance. Less than 3 days before, on the day of arrival or a no-show is not refunded. Read the full policy.
Do you serve food?
We are room only, so meals are not served. Govinda's in the ISKCON campus is a 10 minute walk, and there are prasadam stalls right outside our door. Drinking water is provided.
How many guests can stay in a room?
Each Deluxe Double and Deluxe Twin room takes up to 3 guests. For groups and festival dates, message us and we will plan the rooms for you.
Is the guest house vegetarian?
Yes. Out of respect for Sri Dham Mayapur, no non-veg food, eggs, smoking, tobacco or alcohol are allowed on the premises. There is a ₹2,000 fine for any violation.
Where exactly is the reception?
On the first floor. Enter at the left corner of the building, by the picture of Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu, and take the stairs up. Watch the walk-up video.

A free membership for guests who come back. You get 100 welcome points, 5–20% off every booking, and free nights with your points. See the levels.
